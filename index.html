🏛️ Muziejai (Museums)

Geležinkelių muziejus (Railway Museum)

Vilnius Station €3–€6

Working Hours: Tue–Fri 09:00–17:00

Great indoor spot with interactive displays and real trains outside.

🥾 Pažintiniai takai (Hiking Trails)

Pūčkorių pažintinis takas

15 min from center 3 km long Free

Working Hours: Open 24/7

Beautiful geological exposures, river views, and a nice wooden boardwalk path.
